Behind the Lens

Hello, we're AURELIA.

Founded in 2011 by photographer Elena Marchetti, AURELIA began as a one-woman studio shooting film weddings across the Mediterranean. Today we're a small, deliberately-sized collective of photographers, retouchers and album artisans — working from studios in New York, Paris and Kyoto.

We believe photography should feel like memory: imperfect, warm, and quietly beautiful. No forced poses, no over-processed skies — just light, patience, and an eye trained over thousands of hours to know exactly when to press the shutter.

Elena Marchetti Founder & Lead Photographer
