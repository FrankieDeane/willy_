FINE ART PRINTS

Every photograph is printed on 100% cotton rag with pigment inks, giving a rated colour life of over a century under normal exhibition conditions.

Each print in the Fine Art collection is a limited edition: inspected, dated, numbered and signed by hand, and issued with a certificate of authenticity naming the edition and the photograph.

Prints ship rolled in a rigid tube, or flat and framed, from Buenos Aires. Delivery to the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom and the European Union is typically 7–12 business days, fully tracked and insured.

SIZES

  • 65 × 100 cm26 × 39 in
  • 111 × 165 cm44 × 65 in
  • 150 × 240 cm59 × 94 in

Panoramic formats keep the stated height and vary in length.

PAPERS

Smooth cotton rag
Matte, no optical brighteners. Deep blacks and no surface reflection — the default for architecture and for anything that will hang under gallery lighting.
Baryta fibre
Semi-gloss with a baryta base. Higher micro-contrast and a longer tonal range — chosen for landscape and for high-key work.

FRAMING

Optional museum framing: solid wood in black, natural oak or white, 4 cm archival mount and anti-reflective UV glazing. Framed prints ship flat and crated.

EDITIONS

Editions are small and closed. Once an edition is complete the photograph is not reprinted at that size in any format.

On request Prices depend on size, paper and framing, and are quoted in USD or EUR. Ask for the current price list — there is no obligation.
